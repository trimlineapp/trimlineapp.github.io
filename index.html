How it works

Open, select, save

Trimline does one job: it saves the part of a file you need as a new file, and leaves the original untouched.

  1. Open

    Drop a file on the window or on the Dock icon, or right-click it in Finder and choose Trim in Trimline.

  2. Select

    Drag the yellow handles, or press I and O at the playhead. Click a handle and use the arrow keys to move it frame by frame.

  3. Save

    Press ⌘S, then Return. The clip appears next to the original as Vacation (clip).mov.

Features

Small app, serious engine

Native Swift on top of AVFoundation and a trimmed-down FFmpeg. No Electron, nothing running in the background.

Lossless by default. Nothing to choose.

The video and sound are copied as is, so the clip keeps exactly the same quality and even a large file is usually saved in seconds. MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP are cut to the exact frame. In MKV, AVI, TS and other formats the clip may start a little earlier, and the start handle shows exactly where.

  • Need the exact frame in MKV, AVI, TS and other formats? Turn on Cut video to the exact frame in Settings
  • H.264 and HEVC then usually get a smart cut: only the frames near the cuts are re-encoded, so it still takes seconds. Other video is re-encoded in full, and some older formats are then saved as MKV
  • Audio is copied as is

RealMedia and Monkey’s Audio can be opened but not written, so those clips are saved as Matroska: RealMedia video is re-encoded to HEVC and RealAudio to AAC, and Monkey’s Audio is converted to FLAC without loss. The save sheet says so before you start.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Why does macOS say it can’t verify Trimline?

Trimline is an independent app made without a paid Apple developer account, so it hasn’t been through Apple’s notary service and macOS can’t vouch for it. You allow it once in Privacy & Security, as shown in the install guide.

Trimline is open source, so you can check exactly what it does in the source code: it works only with the files you open and goes online only for updates.

Does Trimline re-encode my video?

No. The video and sound go into the new file without being re-encoded, so nothing is lost.

The Cut video to the exact frame setting changes this for MKV, AVI, TS and other formats; MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP are still cut without re-encoding. With it on, H.264 and HEVC usually get a smart cut: only the frames near the cuts are re-encoded, in hardware, so it still takes seconds. Other video is re-encoded in full, which is slower and loses some quality, and some older formats are then saved as MKV. Audio is copied as is.

Why did the start of my clip move a little?

Without re-encoding, MKV, AVI, TS and some other formats can only start a clip on a key frame, so the start handle snaps to the nearest one and the timeline shows the real start. MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP don’t have this limit.

To cut these formats to the exact frame, turn on Cut video to the exact frame in Settings.

Can I join several pieces or convert to another format?

No. Trimline saves one clip at a time, in the same format as the original. Joining, effects, conversion and batch processing are left out on purpose, to keep the app small and simple.

How is Trimline different from QuickTime Player or LosslessCut?

QuickTime Player can trim too, but only files macOS itself can play: MKV, AVI and many other formats won’t open in it. Trimline opens almost anything and saves the clip in the same format.

LosslessCut is a great free tool with many more features, such as cutting several segments at once, joining files and editing tracks. Trimline does less on purpose: it’s a native Mac app under 30 MB that opens a file and saves one clip, with a Finder menu item and keyboard shortcuts for the whole job.

Does anything leave my Mac?

No. Your files are never uploaded anywhere, and Trimline goes online only to check for and download updates. The details are under Privacy.

“Trim in Trimline” doesn’t show up in Finder

Move Trimline to Applications and open it once. If the item is still missing, run /System/Library/CoreServices/pbs -update in Terminal and relaunch Finder.

Depending on how many actions you have, Finder shows it at the bottom of the menu or under Quick Actions or Services. You can turn it off in System Settings › Keyboard › Keyboard Shortcuts › Services.

Will it run on my Mac?

Trimline needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later and runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel. On macOS 26 it uses the new Liquid Glass look.

AV1 in MP4 uses the hardware decoder on M3 and newer; other AV1 files are decoded by the built-in dav1d.

How do updates work?

Trimline checks for updates on its own and installs them through Sparkle, the standard updater for Mac apps outside the App Store. To check right away, choose Check for Updates… in the Trimline menu.

Updates don’t ask for the Privacy & Security step again. Keep Trimline in Applications: when it runs from the disk image or Downloads, it can’t update itself.

Install

First launch: four steps, once

Trimline is an independent app and hasn’t been through Apple’s notary service, so macOS asks you to allow it the first time. On macOS 15 Sequoia and later that takes the four steps below; updates install without asking again.

  1. Trimline
    Trimline Applications

    Move it to Applications

    Open Trimline.dmg and drag Trimline onto the Applications folder. Always start it from there: when it runs from the disk image or Downloads, it can’t update itself.

  2. “Trimline” Not Opened

    Apple could not verify “Trimline” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.

    Move to TrashDone

    Open it, then click Done

    Double-click Trimline in Applications. macOS says it can’t verify the app. Click Done, not Move to Trash.

    Allow it in Privacy & Security

    Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway. The button stays there for about an hour after you try to open the app.

  4. Open “Trimline”?

    Apple is not able to verify that it is free from malware that could harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.

    Open AnywayDone
    Touch ID or password

    Confirm once

    Click Open Anyway again and confirm with Touch ID or your password. From now on Trimline opens like any other app.

Prefer Terminal? After moving Trimline to Applications, this does the same as steps 2–4:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Trimline.app

On macOS 14 Sonoma it’s simpler: Control-click Trimline in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again.

Privacy

Trimline collects no telemetry and needs no account. Your files never leave your Mac.

The app connects to the internet only to check for and download updates. The check sends no personal data, only the app version in the request, and you can turn automatic checks off in Settings.

This site counts visits and clicks with GoatCounter, without cookies and without collecting personal data.

Open source

Trimline itself is free and open source under the MIT license. The code is on GitHub, and bug reports and ideas are welcome in Issues.

Trimline uses the FFmpeg libraries under the GNU LGPL 2.1. They are linked dynamically and live in Trimline.app/Contents/Frameworks, so you can replace them with your own build. The source code of the exact FFmpeg and dav1d versions in each release, the build script and any patches are available for download.

  • dav1d, AV1 decoder: BSD 2-Clause
  • Sparkle, updates: MIT

Full license texts are in the app under Trimline › About Trimline.
